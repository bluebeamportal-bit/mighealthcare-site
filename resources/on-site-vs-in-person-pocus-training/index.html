On-site, hub-based or blended: choosing a POCUS training format

Once your organization decides to invest in point-of-care ultrasound training, the next question is practical: where and how should it happen? The right format depends on your team's schedule, your space, your equipment and how many people you need to train. Here is how the three main options compare.

On-site training at your facility

Training at your own facility means staff learn on the equipment they will actually use, in the environment where they work. It removes travel time and makes it easier to train around shifts. On-site programs work well for larger groups, department-wide rollouts and teams that cannot easily leave the unit.

The main requirement is space: a quiet room, ultrasound systems for hands-on practice and protected time for participants.

Training at a dedicated training hub

A dedicated training environment removes the distractions of the clinical floor. Participants can focus fully on learning, and instructors can use dedicated teaching equipment and practice models. Hub-based training suits smaller groups, individual clinicians and organizations without suitable training space.

MIG Healthcare's training hub is located at Symphony Towers in downtown San Diego, convenient for healthcare organizations across San Diego County and Southern California.

Blended programs

Blended programs combine short instruction sessions with supervised hands-on practice spread over several weeks. This approach fits busy clinical schedules and supports long-term retention, because skills are practiced repeatedly rather than in a single intensive day. Blended formats are often the best choice for building lasting competency across a department.

Questions to help you choose

  • How many participants need training, and in how many roles?
  • Can staff leave the unit, or does training need to come to them?
  • Do you have ultrasound systems and space available for practice?
  • Do you need a single focused session or a skill that is built over time?
  • How will you document progress for your education and credentialing teams?

How MIG Healthcare delivers training

MIG Healthcare offers all three formats and helps you choose based on your team, space and goals. Many organizations start with a hub-based or on-site pilot, then expand with a blended program. Explore Professional Training and POCUS.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide ultrasound equipment for training?

Equipment needs are confirmed during planning. For on-site programs, we discuss whether your systems, ours, or a combination will be used.

Can you train our team in Los Angeles or Orange County?

Yes. MIG works with healthcare organizations across California and can deliver training at your facility.

What group size works best?

Small groups get more hands-on time per person. We plan group sizes to keep instructor feedback frequent and practice time meaningful.
