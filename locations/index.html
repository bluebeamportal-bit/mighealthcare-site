San Diego · Symphony Towers

An inspiring setting. A purposeful next step.

MIG Healthcare calls Symphony Towers home. Set in the heart of downtown San Diego, its distinctive skyline presence brings a sense of ambition and possibility to the next stage of clinical education.

A professional setting for small-group clinical readiness, coaching and collaboration, Symphony Towers connects our school partners and instructors around a shared standard. Patient-based examinations and hands-on scanning take place at partner clinical sites.
