For the schools that trust us

Your students carry your ambition. We treat it with care.

Behind every cohort is a faculty that has invested in its future. Our role is to complement that work with practical clinical support and a relationship built on clear expectations.

We begin with your priority programs, geography, cohort timing and clinical-capacity needs, and shape everything else around them.

Partnership services

Placement and rotation coordination

Student assignments, rotation schedules, site expectations and capacity, kept connected.

Affiliate relationship management

Weekly communication
