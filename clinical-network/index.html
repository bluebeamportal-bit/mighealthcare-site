What we do

Your outside team for clinical growth.

A dedicated business development function for your clinical program, without hiring one in every market.

Market mapping

Targeted prospect lists of imaging centers, hospitals and outpatient facilities in your priority regions.

Outreach and relationships

Direct outreach that introduces your program and builds real relationships with clinical leaders.

Site qualification

Services offered, exam mix, contrast capability, student capacity per rotation and scheduling availability.

Affiliation coordination

Agreement follow-up, W-9 collection and documentation until each site is ready for your approval.

Capacity planning

Your projected cohorts mapped against available seats, so capacity is ready before students are.

Regular pipeline reporting

Prospects contacted, active conversations, agreements in progress and completed sites.

Built for program leaders

Your program sets the standard. We do the legwork.

Program directors already carry accreditation, faculty and students. Finding and signing new clinical sites in another city, or another state, shouldn't fall on them too.

MIG brings a team that knows imaging centers from the inside, speaks their language, and follows up until every site is ready for your approval.

Proactive, not reactive

Build capacity before students need it.

Share four details for each upcoming cohort and we start developing relationships months ahead.

Where

Location

A clear understanding

MIG does not sign agreements or make commitments on your school's behalf. Clinical affiliation agreements are entered into directly between your school and each site, and your school approves every site.
