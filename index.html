How we work

Allies to the profession. Never in competition.

MIG connects the schools that train imaging professionals with the healthcare organizations that employ them. Each partner keeps what is theirs. We add the capacity, coordination and follow-through.

MRI · Ultrasound · X-ray

Your outside clinical operations team

We act as your clinical coordinators and your business development team, finding and securing the sites your students need.

  • Clinical site development
  • Placement and rotation coordination
  • Clinical readiness training
  • Progress tracking in the Partner Portal
  • Graduate employment support

You keep Academic oversight, site approval, competency assessment and every program decision.

For schools

Stronger imaging teams

Focused POCUS and imaging training for your clinical teams, and a pipeline of trained talent.

  • POCUS education
  • MRI and ultrasound skills training
  • Upskilling and remediation
  • Imaging talent pipeline
  • Partner clinical site program

You keep Clinical oversight and authorization, competency requirements and every hiring decision.
